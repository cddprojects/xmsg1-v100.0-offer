Privacy policy

How this page handles your details

Find Aligner is a career consultancy at 75 Bukit Timah Road, #03-02, Boon Siew Building, Singapore 229833. This notice covers the Singapore offer page and the email enquiry it prepares. It was last updated on 19 November 2026.

What you can send us

The form asks for your name, email, WhatsApp number, whether you are a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident, the hours you can give, the skill you want to practise, an optional note about your week, and your confirmation that you are 25 or older and legally allowed to work.

What stays on your device

Until you send the email, the note sits in this browser’s session storage so the thank-you page can open your mail app. We do not run a form server on this page, and we do not receive the note when you click “Prepare my email”. Sending the email is what shares it with us.

Why we read an email you send

We use it to check eligibility, to decide whether a digital engagement is plausible, and to reply by email or WhatsApp if you asked us to. We do not sell personal data. People inside the consultancy who handle enquiries can read the email. A mailbox provider may store it while delivering the message.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry mail only as long as we need it to assess the request, keep a record of your consent, or meet a legal duty. You can ask us to delete it. We will do that unless a law requires a record to remain.

Your choices

You can ask to access or correct your details, or withdraw consent for further contact, by emailing contact@findaligner.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect use that already happened. You may also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission if you have a concern we do not resolve.
