Side income, done properly

Taking on a second job? Check these first

No Singapore law stops a Citizen or PR from holding a second job. A few simple checks keep your side income smooth and your main job safe.

01

Read your main employment contract

Many contracts include a moonlighting, exclusivity, or conflict-of-interest clause. If yours does, ask your employer for written approval before you start.

02

Public servants need approval first

If you work in a ministry or statutory board, outside employment needs prior approval from your Permanent Secretary or Chief Executive.

03

Keep your day job separate

Work your side role outside your main job’s hours, and never use your employer’s laptop, time, or confidential information for it.

04

CPF applies to each job

Every employer pays CPF on the wages it pays you. If a job pays S$500 or less a month, nothing is deducted from your pay, and your employer still contributes its share.

05

Declare your income to IRAS

Income from an employer outside IRAS’s Auto-Inclusion Scheme needs to be declared in your yearly myTax return.

Not sure where you stand?

Mention your current job when you apply. Your consultant will talk through these checks with you before anything is signed.

FAQ

Have questions? We’ve got answers

How much can I expect to earn?

It depends on your hours and rate. For example, 15 hours a week at S$20 an hour comes to S$300 a week, or about S$1,300 a month before CPF and other deductions. Your exact rate is agreed with you in writing before you start.

Can I do this alongside my full-time job?

Yes, in most cases. No Singapore law stops a Citizen or PR from holding a second job, but check your employment contract for moonlighting or conflict-of-interest clauses and get written approval if needed. Public servants need prior approval from their agency. CPF applies to each job, and side income should be declared to IRAS where required.

What do I need to get started?

Usually a laptop, a stable internet connection, and a quiet place to work. Anything specific to a role is shared with you before you decide.

When do I get paid?

Your payday is shared with you together with your rate, before your first day.
