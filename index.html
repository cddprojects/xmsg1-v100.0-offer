Pay questions

Have questions about the money?

Will I be paid S$28 an hour?

S$28/hr is the top of the example band for Digital Marketing Associate work. The rate you are paid is the rate in your written terms. It can sit inside a band. It can differ. The writing wins.

When does the money arrive?

The payday is written next to the rate, before you start. This page does not set a payment date.

Are these roles open today?

The cards are example bands for the kind of paid work we discuss. A start happens after a fit is confirmed and you accept the terms. Some weeks there is no fit, and we say so.

Who can apply to get paid?

Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents, aged 25 and above, who are legally allowed to work in Singapore.

Is Pogo Hire an employment agency?

No. Pogo Hire is a career consultancy. For an engagement we curate, our stated role is managing partner and employer of that engagement. We do not provide third-party placement.
