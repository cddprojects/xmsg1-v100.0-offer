PDPA notice

Consent for an enquiry

This notice explains the consent checkbox on the Singapore offer form. Read it with the privacy policy. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Data we are asking you to provide

Name, email, WhatsApp number, residency status, weekly availability, the skill you want to practise, any note you add, and your confirmation of age and right to work.

Purpose

To assess whether you are eligible for a conversation about managed digital opportunities, and to contact you by email or WhatsApp about that enquiry.

When collection happens

Preparing the email on this site does not send the data to us. Collection happens when you send the email to contact@findaligner.com, or when you write to us yourself.

Withdrawing consent

Email contact@findaligner.com and ask us to stop using your details or to delete them. We will do so unless the law requires us to keep a record. Our data protection contact is that same address.
